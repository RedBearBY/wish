# Wish

This repository hosts the YaO MVP specification for the Wish project. The high-level summary lives below, while the full product brief with sections 0–14 is documented in [`docs/yao-spec.md`](docs/yao-spec.md).

## YaO MVP at a Glance

- **Vision:** Deliver a guided wish-capturing experience that helps people articulate, organize, and prioritize personal aspirations.
- **Primary users:** Dreamers who want a structured way to record, categorize, and act on their wishes; supporting coaches who help users convert wishes into plans.
- **Core outcome:** Provide a delightful, low-friction workflow for capturing wishes, aligning them to actionable goals, and tracking progress through collaborative tools.

Refer to the linked specification for detailed requirements, success metrics, architecture notes, and release planning.
